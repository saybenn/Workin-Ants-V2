|---|---|---|
| 1 / PRECONDITIONS | Authoritative Offering/Gig/approved screening quote with private tax evidence | Source owner current registered facts and action policy; fixture data never authority |
| 2 / SOURCE_TRANSITION | Order freezes purchase, agreement and seller acceptance before relevant Payment instruction | Actual owner source/history/outbox atomic; no network under source database lock |

**Guards.** Screening source uses subject payer and no fake seller/completed-check prerequisite; no current Track repricing; economic identity distinct from transport dedupe; financial action and every applicable Hold checked at effect boundary; agreement mirrors never authorize

**Failure/recovery.** Unknown money outcome retains reservations and reconciles same effect; no Hold-release dispatch or provider callback foreign write; multi-obligation delivery before completion; partial settlement and corrections retain owner history/currency conservation

**Eventual consistency.** Source revisions and generation tombstones dominate late delivery; consumers recheck current protected scope. Unknown effect reconciles same semantic identity.

Registered source events: order.outcome_changed.v1, payment.effect_recorded.v1, payment.financial_readiness_changed.v1. These are owner facts; source commands/intents/receipts still govern actual effects.
