## Independent service authority, bootstrap and recovery

The private identity_service_authorization currentness leaf and immutable identity_service_receipt are two explicitly registered SQL-only enforcement tables, not extra Prisma domain models or a permission catalog. The existing helper additionally reads only service principal/generation/revoked_at.

The retained identity_authorization_ref is not self-authenticating. That adapter and its protected credentials remain unimplemented owner work; the grant issuer cannot create its own proof of current authority.

1. **Bootstrap:** separate environment authority provisions the narrow Identity connection. The qualified Identity adapter atomically appends a generation-zero bootstrap receipt and current row with no predecessor. Unknown services deny. One transition per service per transaction preserves exact reverse receipt checks.
2. **Rotation:** lock the service key, append a receipt naming the exact old generation/receipt and advance exactly once. Rotate protected credentials under Identity's protocol before fresh grants. Old grants fail currentness.
3. **Revocation:** append revoke receipt, advance generation, server-stamp revoked_at. Identity holds the exclusive service lock; effects use its shared counterpart. Bind takes no action lock, preserving CTX-02's revocation-commit-before-action schedule.
4. **Recovery:** revoked state accepts only an independently authorized recover receipt with exact predecessor/+1. Before restored service connections open, Identity revokes/advances every restored epoch, rotates credentials and supplies fresh recovery decisions. Restored rows/receipts or fresh issuer grants alone cannot prove post-restore authority. Missing external recovery evidence keeps the service closed.

This supplies the missing SQL source; it does not qualify real Identity/credential adapters, race behavior or recovery execution.
